About Penny

PennyUtils (or just Penny) is a feature-rich Discord bot designed to bring interactive music streaming, dynamic meme generation, local AI chatting, and moderation utilities to your server.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 13, 2026

1. What Data We Collect

PennyUtils collects the minimum amount of data necessary to function. We store:

  • Discord User IDs & Guild (Server) IDs: Used to track warnings, custom playlists, favorites, command blacklists, and server configurations.
  • Chat History (Temporary): When interacting with the AI chat feature, the last 10 messages of your conversation are kept in temporary system memory to maintain conversational context. This is cleared upon bot restart or when you use the clear command.
  • Command Usage: We log command usage locally to monitor bot performance and prevent spam/abuse.

2. How We Use Your Data

Data is strictly used to provide the bot's intended features to you (e.g., fetching your saved playlists, applying moderation rules). PennyUtils relies on a local, self-hosted AI model (via LM Studio); your chat data is never sent to third-party corporate APIs like OpenAI or Google.

3. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored in local JSON files (`bot_data.json`, `favorites.json`, `playlists.json`) on the host machine. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties.

4. Data Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your data (User ID, saved playlists, favorites, and warnings). To do so, please contact the bot developer.
